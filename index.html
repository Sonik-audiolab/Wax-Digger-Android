Wax Digger for Android – Privacy Policy

Applies to Wax Digger for Android (package com.audiolab.waxdigger) · Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Wax Digger helps you check the price of vinyl records using the Discogs database. AudioLab does not run its own servers for the app, does not create user accounts, does not show ads and does not sell or share your personal data. Your history and settings stay on your device.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only to read barcodes and the text on record labels. Images are analysed on your device with Google ML Kit and are not uploaded or stored by the app.
  • When you choose a photo from your gallery, the Android photo picker gives the app access only to the photo you select. It is analysed on the device in the same way.
  • Only the text that was read (for example a catalog number, label or artist name) is sent to Discogs as a search request.
  • A hidden diagnostic mode, intended for troubleshooting, can save the analysed label photo to your own photo gallery. It is off by default and only works if you turn it on.

Discogs account

  • Signing in with Discogs is optional. It is used to show Discogs suggested prices for each condition grade.
  • Sign-in happens on the Discogs website. The app receives an access token, which is stored encrypted on your device (Android encrypted storage) and is used only to send requests to the Discogs API.
  • Searches, release details and prices are requested directly from Discogs. Discogs handles that information according to its own Privacy Policy.
  • You can sign out in the app's Settings at any time, and revoke the app's access in your Discogs account settings.

Data stored on your device

  • History and watchlist of the records you checked (title, artist, pressing details, prices, the grade and asking price you entered), used also as an offline cache.
  • Settings: currency, estimated shipping cost and your Discogs username.
  • This data never leaves your device through AudioLab. You can delete the history in Settings (“Clear history”); uninstalling the app removes everything.

Third-party services

  • Discogs – record database, marketplace statistics and price suggestions. Discogs Privacy Policy.
  • Google ML Kit – on-device barcode and text recognition. Google may collect technical information such as device model, operating system version, app information and performance metrics for diagnostics and usage analytics; it is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. See ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play – the app is sold through Google Play; purchases are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. AudioLab does not receive your payment details.

Children

Wax Digger is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
